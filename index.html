About Smoothin'

Founded with a passion for bringing healthier beverage options to life, Smoothin’ was born from a simple observation: in a world full of drinks, very few truly understand what they nourish their bodies with. While modern trends often focus solely on taste, we decided to bring something more meaningful to the table.

Smoothin’ is about more than just flavor; it's about the harmony between indulgence and wellness. We believe that healthy living shouldn't be complicated. By carefully selecting natural fruits and ingredients, we craft smoothies that are as delightful to sip as they are beneficial to your body.

Every sip of Smoothin’ is a small contribution towards a healthier you, a more sustainable environment, and the prosperity of our local farmers. Because we believe great change begins with consistent, small steps.

Through our journey, we discovered three core pillars that we uphold in every Smoothin’ recipe:

Mendukung Literasi Gizi

Masih banyak masyarakat yang belum memahami kandungan gizi dalam makanan dan minuman sehari-hari. Melalui informasi sederhana seperti vitamin, mineral, dan manfaat di setiap produk, Smoothin’ ingin membantu meningkatkan kesadaran gizi secara perlahan dan mudah dipahami.

Gerakan Go Green

Penggunaan plastik sekali pakai masih menjadi salah satu penyumbang limbah terbesar. Dengan program diskon bagi pelanggan yang membawa tumbler sendiri, Smoothin’ mengajak perubahan kecil untuk mengurangi sampah plastik dan mendukung gaya hidup ramah lingkungan.

Mendukung Petani Buah Lokal

Indonesia memiliki kekayaan buah-buahan yang melimpah dan bernutrisi tinggi. Dengan memanfaatkan buah segar, Smoothin’ turut mendukung penggunaan bahan lokal sekaligus menghadirkan produk yang lebih alami dan berkualitas.
